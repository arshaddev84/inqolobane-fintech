# Credit Information Dispute Intake (Synthetic)

- **Case:** CMP-2003
- **Customer:** CUS-1003
- **Account:** ACC-3003
- **Reported issue:** Payment-profile dispute
- **Status:** Investigation in progress

## Customer request
The customer asks the institution to check whether the payment history sent to a credit bureau matches the underlying loan ledger.

## Evidence required
- Account ledger and value-dated payments.
- Payment allocation rules and any suspense/unallocated amounts.
- Credit bureau submission payload and acknowledgement.
- Reporting period, correction history, and applicable dispute process.
- Relevant account terms and customer communications.

## Handling rules
Do not infer that the credit bureau or lender is at fault before comparing source records, transmission events, and bureau output. If an error is confirmed, use the approved correction process and preserve an audit trail.
